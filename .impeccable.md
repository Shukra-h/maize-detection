## Design Context

### Users
The primary users are general demo users viewing an academic maize disease detection project. They may be students, instructors, reviewers, or casual visitors who need to quickly understand what the demo does before trying the image classifier.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel academic, clear, and grounded. It should present the model as a study/demo tool rather than a commercial diagnosis product, with honest language and a calm level of polish.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light field-lab direction: clean academic typography, maize-green accents, restrained notebook-like surfaces, and subtle crop-study visuals. The landing page should be separate from the detector screen and should lead users into the existing upload workflow with a clear CTA.

### Design Principles
- Keep the landing page simple and fast to scan.
- Make the academic/demo nature explicit without making the page feel unfinished.
- Preserve the existing detection workflow and API behavior.
- Use practical, grounded copy that avoids overpromising medical or agronomic certainty.
- Favor calm contrast, readable spacing, and responsive layouts over decorative complexity.
